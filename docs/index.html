Orienteering Belgium – calendar feed

Upcoming orienteering events from O'Punch, refreshed daily as iCalendar (.ics) files you can subscribe to.

  • opunch.ics – all events (local, regional, national)
  • opunch-regional-national.ics – regional + national only
  • opunch-national.ics – national only

Subscribe in Google Calendar

  1. Right-click the feed you want above and copy the link address.
  2. In Google Calendar (web), next to Other calendars click + → From URL.
  3. Paste the link and click Add calendar.

Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription. Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

Google refreshes subscribed calendars roughly every 12–24 h. Cancelled events disappear from O'Punch and therefore from the feed on the next refresh. Not affiliated with O'Punch.
